## Short description

Overall, rental prices are increasing across all boroughs in New York
from the years 2015 to 2024. The borough that experienced the steepest
increase in rental prices is Manhattan with a rent increase of 1056.398,
followed by the Bronx with an increase of 737.301. This could
potentially be due to reasons such as increasing gentrification,
inflation or the general appeal to being close proximity to Manhattan
due to the availability of employment opportunities.
